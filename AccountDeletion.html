Account Deletion Request

If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data from the DREAM app, follow the steps below:

  1. Open the DREAM app on your device.
  2. Log in using your registered parent/guardian account.
  3. Navigate to the Profile section.
  4. Tap on Delete Guardian Account.
  5. Confirm your decision when prompted.

Once confirmed, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your parent/guardian authentication account.
  • All associated children profiles and their data (e.g., name, birthdate, gender).
  • Reports, results, and any Firebase-stored content.

Note: This action is irreversible.
